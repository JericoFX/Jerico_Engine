The project-authored material contains constant StandardPBR properties, with
no parent material or texture references. The WhiteBox renderer's fixed
materials/whiteboxdefault.azmaterial lookup requires this project-local path;
the template's higher project scan-folder priority selects it without modifying
Gem sources. Asset Processor product selection and real rendering are pending.

Original template GameSDK.ico and Splash.bmp are preserved byte for byte after
manual acquisition and SHA256 verification. Example models/HDR images remain
excluded. The original engine Editor splashscreen_background.png is unavailable
(HTTP 404); no substitute is provided. StandardPBR/Atom shader, font and other
engine asset closure has not been cooked or visually validated by this project
preparation. The native action/shape ports retain upstream O3DE license notices;
historical scripts are preserved outside the fixture's product execution path.
